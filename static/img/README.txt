PUT YOUR COLLEGE LOGO HERE
==========================

Save your official logo as:   logo.png   (in this folder: static/img/logo.png)

- It appears at the top of every printed fee challan and can be used in the
  dashboard header.
- PNG with a transparent background works best.
- Recommended height around 150-200px; width is flexible.
- If no logo.png is present, the challan falls back to a simple "B" badge.

That's the only place you need to put the image. Nothing else to configure.
